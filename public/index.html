THE RECIPE FOR AGREEMENT

One table. One decision.

  1. Make a room.

    Set your group size, budget, and distance. Share the invite link.

  2. Get everyone ready.

    Each person joins on their own device. The host starts when the whole table is ready.

  3. Go with your gut.

    Vote privately on every meal. A match needs a yes from everyone. If several match, the first meal in the shared deck wins.

This first edition uses a sample menu, illustrative venues, and distances. Directions searches for nearby restaurants of the winning cuisine; it is not a booking or a verified venue listing.
